01 — Overview

Meet KYL

Android app

Know Your Label helps you read food labels and understand how a product fits your needs. Take or upload a photo to analyze ingredients and nutrition, highlight possible allergy and diet conflicts, and explore clear explanations of food scores and concerns.

A photo has limits. The app assists with label reading; a photo cannot confirm that food is allergy-safe.

02 — Inside the app

A closer look

Scan · Understand · Ask
01 · Capture

Scan your food label

  • AI food scanning: Take or upload a photo to analyze ingredients and nutrition.
  • Photo guidance: Check lighting, blur, and glare, with guidance on when a label needs another photo.
02 · Understand

Read beyond the score

  • Clear results: Explore food scores, concerns, and the evidence read from your photo.
  • Ask the AI: Ask follow-up questions about an analyzed product.
03 · Personalize

Check against your needs

  • Personalized checks: Highlight possible allergy and diet conflicts.
  • Allergy and diet profiles: Choose existing profiles or describe custom needs with AI assistance.
04 · Keep track

Your scans, your way

  • Scan history: Browse, search, save, and delete previous scans.
  • Account access: Sign in with Google or email.
  • Appearance: Choose light or dark themes.
05 · Stay current

Updates on your terms

  • Notifications: Optional beta announcements, early-feature updates, and local reminders.
  • App updates: View release notes and download the universal Android APK.
06 · Take part

Help shape the app

  • Feedback: Submit beta ratings and bug reports.
  • Donations: Support the project through UPI.
